# Phase 07.2 Plan 01: Schema 3 of the attempt record Summary

**Attempt-log schema 3: the driver writes every record as schema 3 with effort, harness, backend, duration_ms, cost_source, giveup and hold_run. HOLD_EXACT (hold runs only, never MATCHED), the capped D-04 give-up object and a cost-free landing record validate with their rules. flags and stats read v3 and landings. Haiku 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 are in the three model tables, and `stats --run` prints a model x size-bucket table.**

## Accomplishments

- Schema 3 in driver.py: `SCHEMA_V3`, `RECORD_FIELDS_V3` for attempt, abort, run, limit, rename and landing. `_record_fields` routes v1, v2 and v3, and every writer (`_base`, `_record_attempt`, `_record_limit`, `_record_run`, `_record_rename`) writes `SCHEMA_V3`, so a night never mixes shapes.
- The attempt record now keeps `Usage.duration_ms` (null without a result event) and says whether its cost is the CLI's (`cli`) or the stream estimate (`estimate`). Nights write `effort: null`, `harness: "claude"`, `backend: "anthropic"`, `giveup: null`, `hold_run: false`.
- D-26 hold-run rules: `HOLD_EXACT` is a schema-3 matching outcome valid only with `hold_run` true. A hold run is never MATCHED ("a hold run never lands"). `held` is refused as an extra key.
- `GIVEUP_CATEGORIES` (the nine D-04 names, pinned by a test) and `_GIVEUP` (five keys, texts non-empty and at most `_TEXT_CAP`, `best_code` a `checks/NNNN.c` path or null).
- `KIND_LANDING` with exactly the RULINGS keys and no tokens or cost. `_landing_problem`: a MATCHED landing names its commit and no reason, a REFUSED one its reason and no commit.
- Schema-3 run totals count sessions over the v3 outcomes, HOLD_EXACT included, with `parked`. The v2 `_totals_shape` is unchanged.
- flags: `_attempt_models` reads v2 and v3 alike (RESEARCH Pitfall 4). `pilot_hits` takes a MATCHED landing as a match.
- stats: `v3_records` apart from `v2_records`; a MATCHED landing is a logged match whose cost is counted once, in the held attempt.
- `run_selected` (a trailing `*` is a prefix, for 07.2-06's `--land-held` too) and `collect(runs=...)`: a landing is kept by its run or its from_run, and `matched` comes from the runs' own records.
- `by_model_size` with its "per model and size" lines, and `reverseapoc stats --run RUN` (repeatable).
- `claude-haiku-5-5` and `claude-sonnet-5-5` in `MODEL_NAMES` (`HAIKU_MODEL`, `SONNET_MODEL`), `ACCEPTED_INIT_MODELS` and `PRICES_PER_MTOK`. A test pins that every accepted id has a price and a co-author name.

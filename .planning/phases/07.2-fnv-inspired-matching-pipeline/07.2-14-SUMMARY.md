## Issues Encountered

- **The round trips cannot show the old form's fault.** With no call in the frame, the old `char[4]` drafts of `pr_lift_taken_x3` and `_byte` also compiled back to the probe bytes. The compiler kept M2 for three `char[4]` there, while `pr_arr_char4_x3`, which passes the arrays to a call, leaves it. So array placement depends on more than declarations, which is why the model refuses those frames. The round trips prove the new form, and the RED is the lifter unit tests.
- **A `-oneatx` lifter limit, out of scope.** Under `-oneatx`, a `char[4]` original with byte stores (`pr_lift_taken_chars`) builds its byte constants in one register, and the lifter writes that as a mask, `(((33 & -65281) | ((34 & 255) << 8)) >> 8) & 255`, which does not compile back. The case runs in plain only. It is noted here and in `probe/cg_lift.c`, and not fixed.
- **Done gate.** Host `uv run pytest -q` gave 6166 passed and 41 skipped, with no failure. `uv run ruff check .` was clean. Container `docker compose run --rm test` gave 6205 passed and 2 skipped, with no failure. Ruff in the container was clean. They ran one after the other. I saw no failure apart from the intended RED runs listed above.
- **`ruff format --check` is not part of the gate.** It reports pre-existing differences in `tests/test_lift.py` and `tests/test_slots_real.py`, all of them in code this plan did not touch. I left them alone.

## Shared Patterns

- **Refusal = exit 2 with a named cause:** `AttemptError` → `cli.py:1554-1556`; CLI tools print
  `reverseapoc <verb>: error: ...` to stderr and return `EXIT_TOOL` (`cli.py:2330-2332`). Applies to plans 02-06, 08 and 12.
- **One predicate for hook and audit:** `hooks.matching_write_allowed` (`hooks.py:671`) reused by `driver._strays`
  (`driver.py:2791-2800`, `:2912-2928`). Applies to every new session-written file (02, 03) and the Codex/agy audit (13, 15).
- **Additive schema, never rewrite lines:** `_totals_shape` (`driver.py:676-686`). Applies to plan 01 and to any later
  reader.
- **Docstrings cite the decision ids** (`D-xx`, `quick 2611xx-yyy`, `T-07-nn`), and `# fmt: skip` sits on hand-wrapped
  calls. Applies to all Python.
- **Fail closed on unknown models:** `coauthor` (`driver.py:307-315`), `check_init` (`streamjson.py:368-405`).
  Applies to plans 01, 12, 13 and 15.
- **No new deps; stdlib `tomllib`** for backends.toml (YAGNI, `.claude/rules/tools.md`).

Gap closure for the design question that 07-25 and 07-28 raised. The owner ruled on it on 2026-10-30, and the ruling
is binding: "A later tier's entry may change an earlier tier's role projection (a row going from closure to hub)
WITHOUT re-signing the earlier tier." The fix: a tier's digest covers its must-match membership only, not the
hub/closure projection, and config/mechanics-members.csv gains a hub column, so that `mechanics check` (no game files)
can tell which entry rows are hubs. Tiers 1-3 are then re-signed once, at an owner checkpoint (F36). After that, 07-32
(Tier 4) and later re-drafts may use entries that today would reopen an earlier digest.

Digest scope, decided here and justified against D-04 and T-07-28:
- **Kept.** The hub-rule line, the tier's must-match rows with their tier-N member items, and 07-10's TOML lines:
  - items of every scope;
  - their entries, with evidence, detail and source;
  - hand edges from any row a tier-N item reaches.
  These are what D-04 says the owner signs: items, entries, their evidence and the call-tree evidence the owner proved
  by hand. They also include the must-match set that the D-05 done rule counts. So T-07-28 (tampering with a signed
  tier) still fails CI on every change the owner ruled on.
- **Dropped.** The hub/closure/asm labels and the rows that have no tier-N member item (the D-02 full-closure list).
  These are derived from the signed entries, the call graph and the signed hub rule. A later tier's entry relabels
  them without changing anything the owner ruled on. They stay guarded by read_members' exact-format and agreement
  checks, by the new D-31 check, and by the real drift test on main (T-07-126).

**Pure functions, so `--self-check` can run them on canned records:**
- `outcome_of(records)`: a row's cell records to one outcome. Exact when any cell is; `near` when the best raw
  `score` of a compiled cell is >= `NEAR`; `compiled` when one compiled; else `compile-failed`. Refused and crash rows
  come from the lift step and have no record. The raw score decides `near`, never a stack-masked one.
- `table(rows)`: outcome by bucket with totals, for not-matched rows. Matched rows get their own line (exact, compiled,
  refused): `matched` is a status, not an outcome.
- `moved(previous, current)`: true when `exact` or `near` changed on either number (D-27).
- `changes(previous, current)`: each of those four numbers that changed, with its sign, such as `s near -2`. It reports
  and counts nothing: a loss in `near` is still movement under D-27, but it now reads as a loss.
- `stop_line(point, flat_streak)`: `base` at point 0; through points 1 to 3 it names the stop rule, "stop the phase after
  two plans in a row that move nothing", and returns exactly `STOP` when the streak is 2 or more; at points 4 to 6 it
  returns `exempt (D-27)` and the streak does not grow. A flat point adds 1 to the streak and a moved point resets it to 0.
- `summary_row(cells)`: one line of the summary table, `| a | b |`, each cell written as given. Nothing else builds that
  line.

## Decisions Made

See `key-decisions` above. Two more, with their reasons:

- A hole clears the flags, so a jump after a hole is a hole too. A stale flag source would write a wrong condition silently.
- `measure_curve.py` retries on `dict(sigs)`. A strict lift that stops early reaches fewer callees than a hole lift, and `sigs` is order-sensitive, so a shared dict would let a retry change a later row's strict lift.

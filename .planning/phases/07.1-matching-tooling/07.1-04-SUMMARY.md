## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Equal token lists were class slot under the plan's step 2**
- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** R10d takes equal token lists out of `tail` and does not say where they go. Step 2 then calls them `slot`, because they are trivially equal under the stack mask, and routes them to the search. No stack offset differs in such a row, so the label is false, and a declaration search cannot fix a relocation.
- **Fix:** with equal tokens the masks are skipped, so the pair is `size` when its byte lengths differ and `mixed` when they do not. A relocation-only row then routes on its score, to the converter at 90 or more.
- **Files modified:** `tools/reverseapoc/classify.py`, `tests/test_classify.py`
- **Verification:** `test_equal_tokens_are_not_class_tail`, `test_equal_tokens_of_different_lengths_are_class_size`, and the `equal-tokens-to-ladder` mutant
- **Committed in:** `d7e8c1a4`
- **Effect:** 0 of the 1836 point-3 records are compiled, not exact and at score 100, so this labels no row of that data.

**2. [Rule 1 - Bug] The plan's second truth and its steps 3 to 6 disagree on a different frame**
- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** the truth says a row with a different frame is class `size`. The steps only check the frame at step 2, so a row with another frame and a register difference would read `register`, and one where nothing fits would read `mixed`.
- **Fix:** the frame check runs right after `tail`, before any mask. The truth holds everywhere. `test_stack_and_register_together_are_class_register` keeps equal frames, so the cumulative masks still decide that case.
- **Files modified:** `tools/reverseapoc/classify.py`, `tests/test_classify.py`
- **Verification:** `test_a_different_frame_is_class_size_whatever_else_differs`, and the `frame-check-off` mutant
- **Committed in:** `d7e8c1a4`
- **Effect:** this moves class labels only, and never a route: `size`, `register`, `branch` and `mixed` all route the same. In the spot check, 2 of the 61 `size` records have equal byte lengths, the set step 5 alone would have called `mixed`.

### CR-03: `_class_of` cuts filler from the original's side only, so real rows that read `register`, `branch` or `slot` now read `size`

**File:** `tools/reverseapoc/classify.py:164-166`, `docs/toolchain.md:416`

**Issue:** the fix rests on WR-03's premise that our function carries no filler. `omf2elf._code_end` trims only the eax-only `x86.is_filler`, and only down to a terminal instruction, so `defined.ours` keeps any tail holding filler of another register or form, such as `lea edx, [edx]` or `mov ebx, ebx`. Ran through `class_of_object`, with `lea edx, [edx]` after `ret` on both sides:

|---|---|---|---|

| `body(MOVE_EBX_TO_EAX)`, `body(MOVE_EBX_TO_EDX)` | `register` | `size` | `register` |

So the fix creates the defect it was written to remove: a slot-only pair that used to route to `search` no longer does. Truth 4 ("a row's trailing filler changes no class") fails, and `docs/toolchain.md:416`'s new wording is false, because these rows have equal lengths on both sides and a mask explains them.

Ran read-only over `build/batch/run/`, 1,670 objects that differ from their row. Our side ends in filler in 63 of 838 `committed` objects and 398 of 832 `cell-1` (`-oneatx`) objects, the cell that gets the pad. Whole-row class to shipped class, on objects where ours holds filler:

|---|---|---|

No object reads `slot` under either reading today, so routes do not move on this data. But Task 6 says any class change outside its list is a bug to explain, and this is outside it: the list holds one object to `tail` and nothing from `register` to `size`. Point 7 will show it.

**Fix:** cut both sides, and keep "equal once cut" as `tail`.


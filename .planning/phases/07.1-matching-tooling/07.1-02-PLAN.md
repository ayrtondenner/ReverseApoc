make the search use it (D-02).

D-27.

Purpose: plan 1 permutes declarations blindly. Research found that memory locals follow a small rule (size, then
declaration, padded to 4, with scope changing the order) while register-candidate scalars ignore declarations entirely.

`probe/` cannot calibrate this today: none of its 7 functions forces a local into memory, and `FLAG_SETS` has no
`-4r -oneatx` entry. This plan writes that C. YAGNI never applies to it (D-19), so `probe/cg_slots.c` gets every shape the
rules need.

first verdict on the YAGNI rule.

Spike facts this plan relies on (04-RESEARCH.md "Spike results", verified 2026-10-21): wibo 1.2.0 runs 10.6 and
11.0 BINNT compilers from a read-only tree; the 10.x BINNT loader opens `..\BINW\WCC386.EXE` relative to its own

give rc 1/2 (10.6) or 8 (11.0) and write no object; without `-fr=` the compiler writes `<stem>.err` into the cwd;
`WCC386=-3r` in the environment changes codegen; the NT-hosted compilers predefine `__NT__` unless `-bt=dos`.

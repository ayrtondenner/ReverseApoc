# An errata ledger that stats applies to re-run or corrected nights

**Why.** Re-run nights, mis-attributed costs and corrected records are explained only in prose (learnings, model-comparison).

**Do.** A committed progress/errata.toml (run, field, correction, reason, date) that stats applies, and lists, when it reports a run.

**Done when.** stats --run shows the correction and its reason; the paper's errata table is generated from it.

**Effort / value.** S-M / med-high. Evidence and sources: docs/research/notes-ideas-tooling-reproducibility.md #7.
